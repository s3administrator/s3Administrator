The toolkit

Everything you reach for, in one place.

The day-to-day of working in object storage, without juggling the AWS console, the CLI, and three web dashboards.

Multi-credential, multi-bucket

Connect AWS, Hetzner, R2, MinIO, Storadera and more, then switch between accounts and buckets without leaving the app.

Full object control

Upload, download, move, rename, delete — plus folder operations, recursive delete, and batch actions across thousands of keys.

Background tasks

Copy, move, sync, and migrate run in parallel and resume where they left off — close the window and they keep their place.

Gallery & global search

Image and video thumbnails for visual buckets, plus instant search across every indexed file in every bucket.

Encrypted at rest

Credentials are sealed with AES-256-GCM using a key derived per install. Your keys never leave your machine.

Fully local, fully private

No account, no cloud, no telemetry. Metadata lives in an embedded PostgreSQL on your own disk.

Private by design

Your keys. Your data. Your machine.

S3 Administrator never phones home. There's no sign-up and no backend to leak. It boots its own embedded PostgreSQL, encrypts your credentials locally, and talks only to the storage providers you point it at.

How it works

Three steps to control.

01

Add a credential

Paste an access key for AWS, Hetzner, R2, or any S3 endpoint. It's encrypted and stored locally on the spot.

02

Browse your buckets

Every bucket and object appears instantly — folders, sizes, thumbnails, and search across the whole index.

03

Move data at will

Drag, sync, or migrate between providers. Long jobs run in the background and pick up exactly where they stopped.
